# 📌 Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end retail analytics solution developed using Blinkit sales data to analyze revenue performance, customer behavior, product trends, and operational insights.

The project focuses on transforming raw retail data into meaningful business insights through SQL analysis, Python exploratory data analysis, and an interactive Power BI dashboard.

The dashboard was designed using a modern Blinkit-inspired interface with KPI cards, trend analysis, category insights, city-level sales analysis, and executive-style reporting visuals.

---

# 🎯 Business Objective

Blinkit operates in a highly competitive quick-commerce industry where product performance, customer satisfaction, and delivery efficiency directly influence business growth.

This project helps analyze:

- Product category performance
- Revenue contribution trends
- Brand-wise sales performance
- City-level revenue insights
- Discount impact on revenue
- Customer rating behavior
- Retail business intelligence metrics

The goal of this project is to create a professional business dashboard that supports data-driven retail decision-making.

# 📈 Key Business Insights

## Revenue Analysis
- Bakery and Grocery categories contributed the highest revenue
- Mumbai and Bengaluru were among the top-performing cities
- Top brands generated a significant share of total sales

## Customer Insights
- Highly rated products generally achieved stronger sales performance
- Certain product categories maintained consistently better customer engagement

## Discount Analysis
- Moderate discounts improved sales volume
- Heavy discounting reduced profit margins
- Some premium products performed strongly without aggressive discounts

## Category Performance
- Product category contribution varied significantly across brands
- Grocery, Bakery, and Household products dominated revenue contribution
